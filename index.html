About THE AD DIGITAL

Terms & Policy

Clear terms. Professional working relationship.

Please review these conditions before confirming a package.

  1. Advance payment: After the package is finalized, the client must pay 35% of the final package price as an advance before work begins.
  2. Cancellation: Once the package has been finalized, cancellation during the package period is not permitted. If the client cancels, any payment already made is non-refundable.
  3. Work completed before cancellation: If work has already been performed and the scope/value of completed work is higher than the amount paid, the client must pay the applicable amount for the work completed.
  4. 60% completion payment: Once approximately 60% of the agreed work is completed, the remaining applicable package payment becomes due according to the agreed arrangement.
  5. Boosting / advertising payment: Before an advertisement is run, the client must pay the required ad budget for the selected amount and duration. Ads will be run after the required payment is received.
  6. Extra work: If the client requests work beyond the agreed package scope, the additional work will be charged separately according to the per-work cost finalized by THE AD DIGITAL.
  7. Trust, conduct & misuse: Both parties are expected to provide accurate information and maintain a professional working relationship. If a client intentionally breaches trust, misuses the service, commits fraud, or causes unlawful harm to THE AD DIGITAL, the agency reserves the right to take appropriate legal action. The client must not interfere with or misuse the agency's work, team, systems or business operations.
  8. Scope clarity: Package deliverables should be confirmed before the project starts. Custom requests, major revisions or services outside the agreed scope may require an additional charge.
